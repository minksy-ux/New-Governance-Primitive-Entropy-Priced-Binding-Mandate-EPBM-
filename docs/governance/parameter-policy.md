# Governance Parameter Policy

## Objective
Define allowed ranges, review cadence, and escalation policy for EPBM parameters.

## Guardrails
- Bound changes by pre-defined max step sizes.
- Require simulation evidence before major changes.
- Require cooldown between parameter updates.

## Required Evidence for Changes
1. Capture simulation deltas.
2. Expected quorum/passage/veto impact.
3. Risk assessment and rollback plan.
